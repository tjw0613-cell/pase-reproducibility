Figure S1. Gating changes relative to detection-matched genes. Panels A–C show GSE234933, GSE181919 and GSE310797 using 48, 18 and 15 fixed patients, respectively. Curves show empirical cumulative distributions of the equal-patient mean gated-minus-ungated correlation changes, with the distinct dashed line and point locating LILRB4 and a reference line at zero. Correlations use the same pre-gate 15-gene score and depth adjustment; gating requires positive counts for at least 2 outcome genes. Of 200 selected controls per cohort, 197, 198 and 196 were estimable across all fixed patients, and the incomplete controls remain in the accompanying data. LILRB4's signed midrank percentiles are 95.9, 99.5 and 85.7, respectively; these are descriptive positions among measured genes, not P values or a biological null distribution.

Figure S2. Native count benchmarks under equal and unequal cell allocation. Panels A–C show the generating null, fixed-positive and heterogeneous-positive scenarios, respectively. Hollow points denote 80 cells per patient; solid points denote unequal yields of 20–260 cells independent of patient effects. Each scenario contains 500 attempted 8-patient cohorts per allocation; points show two-sided rejection rates over all attempts, with pointwise 95% Wilson intervals. The dashed reference line in A marks 5%; A spans 0–10%, whereas B and C span 0–100%. All methods returned P values for every cohort, but memento retained different numbers of patients (Table S4G). Alternative rejection rates concern distinct targets and uncertainty procedures, and total cell counts were not matched between allocations.
