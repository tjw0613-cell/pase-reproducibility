Figure 2. Donor dependence and sensitivity to unequal cell contributions. (A) Retrospective Gaussian controls have eight donors, independent gene-specific donor shifts and zero within-donor association; each allocation uses 2,000 datasets, with pointwise 95% Wilson intervals and a nominal 5% reference line. (B) The largest donor's cells are repeated ten times in total while processed scores and donor identities are held fixed. The primary analyses include 48 of 51 attempted donors in GSE234933, 19 of 20 in GSE181919 and 15 of 16 in GSE310797. Circles show original estimates, diamonds show estimates after duplication, and lines join the paired values.
