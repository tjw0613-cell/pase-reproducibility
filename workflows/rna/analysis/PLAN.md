# Patient-level RNA analysis

This retrospective revision uses the published Li et al. 2021 SPP1-positive tumour-associated macrophage gene list. Source definition, adaptation, derivation cohort and hashes are recorded in ../signature. The design is fixed locally before estimating this new outcome. Previous cohort results and unrelated benchmark analyses were known. This is not a prospective registration.

The primary endpoint is the arithmetic mean of within-patient standardized log expression of the published genes. Freeze the intersection of unambiguously measured genes across all technically readable input specimens before examining associations. Require at least 80% of the published list; use the identical panel and denominator everywhere. Exact gene symbols are matched case-insensitively; ambiguous duplicate names are omitted globally. The anchor and adjustment genes must be outside the panel. A measured constant gene contributes zero after centering, and an entire constant score is unavailable. A missing gene row is not an observed zero.

Use all deposited author-labelled myeloid cells (GSE234933 and GSE310797 “Myeloid cells”; GSE181919 “Macrophages” or “Dendritic.cells” in primary cancers). These mappings do not guarantee identical cell-type composition. Include zero-count target genes; exclude cells whose total library across the full deposited gene axis is zero. Compute log1p(CP10K). Within each selected specimen, standardize each signature gene over all positive-library, broad-myeloid cells before any detection gate using the sample standard deviation; average on a fixed denominator. Every model requires at least 30 retained cells, sufficient residual degrees of freedom and variation in the residual anchor and outcome. Use an intercept, removing constant adjustment columns. The primary adjustment is log1p(total library). Fit LILRB4 and the signature on the same nuisance matrix in separate OLS regressions; correlate residual vectors.

One factor at a time: (i) unadjusted; (ii) depth plus log-normalised CD68, a non-endpoint macrophage marker; (iii) select cells detecting at least two signature genes after score construction; (iv) for GSE234933 only, replace the earlier recurrence HN23 with HN45 or exclude both (one donor). Form the selected donor set before checking cell eligibility, and never substitute a different specimen after measurement failure. Primary uses HN23. The alternative TREM2 anchor uses the primary model, with a paired descriptive LILRB4-minus-TREM2 mean difference on the common eligible set. TREM2 results belong in the supplement.

Each attempted patient remains in a ledger. Report the equal arithmetic mean with a working two-sided Student t interval across eligible patients, and the restricted maximum likelihood random-effects mean of Fisher-transformed correlations with a classical HKSJ interval back-transformed to the correlation scale. For the latter use 1/(n − k − 3) as the working Fisher variance, with k = measured nuisance rank minus intercept. Respect the distinct targets. Report counts, direction, pointwise intervals and weights. Insufficient or degenerate fits withhold the corresponding interval; no nominal type I interpretation for post-result sensitivity contrasts. No cutoff for universal validity is inferred from the eligible count. Provide sample medians as optional descriptions only.

On primary estimable patients, retain naive pooled-cell Pearson correlations, Pearson correlations of stacked within-patient residuals, the actual cell share and Fisher weight for each donor, and leave-one-donor-out change for the unweighted pooled cell correlation and equal/weighted estimates. Select the donor with the largest eligible cell count before inspecting associations (lexicographic ID ties). Replicate all its cells to ten times the original number, keeping its ID fixed; hold processed scores fixed (within-patient standardized genes differ only by a common multiplicative scaling if recomputed). This is a sensitivity to patient representation, not new independent observations. The raw pooled and residual-stacked correlations differ in target. When a change is small report it at its measured size.
