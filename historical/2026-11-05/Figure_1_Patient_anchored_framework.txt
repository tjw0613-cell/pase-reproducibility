Figure 1. Targets and workflow of patient-anchored association estimation. (A) The same illustrative cells are pooled into one cell-weighted estimate or retained within donors to obtain one association per donor, followed by an explicit aggregation target. (B) Equal-patient inference targets the mean observed donor correlation, random-effects inference targets a population Fisher mean, and empirical Bayes provides individual donor estimates conditional on fitted population parameters. (C) Evaluation combines completed simulation and method controls with a fixed published SPP1-positive macrophage signature in three RNA cohorts. Attempted and eligible donors, uncertainty, analysis availability and donor influence are reported alongside the estimates.
