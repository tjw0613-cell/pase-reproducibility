Figure 5. Method benchmarks and computation. (A) Gaussian tests use eight patients and 2,000 datasets per allocation and scenario, with a shared global null and method-specific alternatives. (B) Reference calls use four gene pairs from 19 cohort B patients and require a positive estimate with two-sided P < 0.05; the shuffle column reports rejections in 999 LILRB4–MMP14 shuffles. (C) Runtime and peak process-tree memory are medians of three successful runs, with one attempt per timeout and asterisks marking memory at timeout; PASE returns all summaries and memento returns patient moments. (D) The native count benchmark reports rejected and usable fits from 100 attempted datasets per scenario for PASE and memento and 12 for scCOSMiX.
